Welcome to Mike OS: Mike DeMar's portfolio, built as a little computer you can explore.

## Getting around

- **Double-click** a folder or file to open it (on a phone, just **tap**). Folders open in Files; documents open in Notes.
- In a document, choose a heading from **Contents** to jump to it, and click a picture to see it larger.
- **Drag** a window by its title bar. Resize it from the box in its bottom-right corner.
- The boxes in a title bar close, zoom and collapse the window. On a phone, close takes you back.
- On a tablet, **press and hold** an icon for more options.
- Open **Control Panel** from the menu in the top-left corner to change the desktop pattern, the clock, the screensaver and sounds.

## Using a keyboard

- **Tab** reaches the menu bar, then the desktop.
- In the menu bar, **← →** move between menus and **↓** opens one.
- With a window's title focused, the **arrow keys** move it and **Shift + arrows** resize it.
- **Ctrl + `** switches between open windows.
- **Special → Reset Desktop** puts every icon and window back where it started.
